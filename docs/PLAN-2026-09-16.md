# The shelf becomes a library — plan, 2026-09-16

Owner's brief, in one paragraph: turn `works.html` (today a text grid of the
49 works the collection quotes) into a beautiful, minimal bookshelf that
replaces his Goodreads account. Real front covers. Books lift with depth on
hover and open to their details and quotes. The shelf can be re-sorted and the
books glide to their new places. Goodreads' four shelves (read, currently
reading, want to read, did not finish) and all 87 of his books arrive with
their dates. He rates any book 0–10: for a book he has read that is how good it
was, for one he has not it is how much he wants to read it. Light and dark,
Danish and English. Fonts refreshed from his font library where that is an
improvement. The shelf and "add a quote" reachable from the top of the front
page, the shelf prominently, add-quote small. Favouriting works inside focus
mode. Animations in the spirit of Google's Material motion and Apple's: a star
pops when he rates, focus mode's next/previous glides. No "liquid glass". And
curation without opening GitHub, if it can be done.

The quality bar he named: his Video Library (https://sendefladen.pages.dev,
Material 3 tokens, spring-physics controls) and the Books UI screenshot he
pasted: warm cream page, a large featured book with a soft drop shadow standing
on a shelf ledge, a serif display headline, pill navigation, cards with gentle
radius, a tan/gold accent, small star ratings, outline "Buy now" pills, one book
tilted at the shelf's end. Minimal, spacious, professional.

## Decisions (made 2026-09-16, say so if you deviate)

**One registry.** `data/works.json` stays the single record of every work,
quoted or not. Goodreads books merge into it. New optional fields per work:

| field | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `shelf` | `read`, `reading`, `to-read`, `abandoned`, or absent (non-book works, and books that are quoted but were never on his Goodreads) |
| `rating` | 0–10, how good it was. Only meaningful when `shelf` is `read` or `abandoned`. Seeded from Goodreads stars × 2 where he had rated (21 of 87). |
| `want` | 0–10, how much he wants to read it. Only meaningful when `shelf` is `to-read` or `reading`. Starts empty. |
| `cover` | repo path, `assets/covers/<slug>.webp`, max 800px tall, ≤ 120 KB. Absent for non-books; the page draws a typographic cover for those. |
| `coverSource` | where the picture came from, e.g. `openlibrary:14812834` or `goodreads:204927599`, so a wrong cover can be traced. |
| `pages` | page count, from Goodreads. |
| `goodreads` | `{ "id": "…", "isbn": "…", "url": "https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/<id>" }` |
| `added` | ISO date the book entered his library (Goodreads `user_date_added`; for quoted-only works, the earliest `addedAt` among its quotes). |
| `read` | ISO date finished, when Goodreads had one (7 of 87). |

Two fields for the score rather than one, because a single number would
silently change meaning the day a book moves from "want to read" to "read".

The existing validator's warning "in the registry but has no quotes" is
suppressed for any work with a `shelf`: a library is allowed to hold books
nobody has quoted yet.

**Non-book works** (interviews, essays, speeches, documents, 25 of 49) stay on
the shelf, in their own section "Talks & essays", with generated typographic
covers. They have no shelf status.

**The write path.** A tiny Cloudflare Worker (`worker/`, free tier, his
account, D1 for storage) accepts rating, want, shelf, favourite and new-quote
writes from the site, gated by a secret the site remembers per device. The site
reads the Worker's state on load and lays it over the repo JSON, so a change is
visible instantly on every device. A scheduled GitHub Action pulls that state
into `data/works.json` and `data/quotes.json` every few hours, so the repo
remains the archive and the plain-text promise of the README holds. If the
Worker is unreachable the site still renders from the repo, favourites fall
back to this device, and edit controls say so instead of failing silently. No
GitHub personal token anywhere: the Action uses its own built-in token, and the
Worker never writes to GitHub.

**Themes.** The four editions stay (paper is light, night is dark; folio and
index remain). The shelf must look right in all four. No blur or glass
effects: the sticky bar becomes solid.

**Language.** `assets/i18n.js`, modelled on the Video Library's: a dictionary
keyed by id with `en` and `da` side by side, `t(key, vars)`, `setLang()`,
remembered per browser, default English. Pages register their own strings with
`register({...})` so two builders never edit the same dictionary. Quotes,
titles and author names are never translated. A `DA`/`EN` toggle sits in the
header of every page.

**Fonts.** Chosen by a rendered comparison, not by name. Candidates from his
library, subset to Latin + Danish and served as separate woff2 files with
`font-display: swap` (the current 778 KB base64 stylesheet goes). The night
edition loses Spectral, which is not in the library.

**Motion.** Sorting uses FLIP (measure, reorder, invert, play) so every book
glides to its place; the emphasized easing `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` at
420–560 ms with a small per-item stagger. Hover lifts a book with a 3D tilt and
a deeper shadow. Opening a book uses the View Transitions API where available
and a scale-from-card fallback. Rating pops the chosen star (scale overshoot,
short rotation, a ring that fades). Focus mode's next/previous slides the
outgoing line out and the incoming one in along the direction of travel.
Everything honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Reach from the front page.** A nav row under the masthead on `index.html`:
Quotes · **Shelf** · Board · Read next, with Shelf visibly the primary
destination (a filled pill or a book glyph), and a small `+` "Add" beside it.
The sticky controls row also carries a Shelf link, so it is reachable while
scrolled. Add-quote opens an in-page form when editing is unlocked, and falls
back to the prefilled GitHub issue when it is not.

## What is out of scope today
Goodreads reviews, reading challenges, friends, and re-syncing from Goodreads
after this migration (he is leaving Goodreads; the daily quote sync from his
Goodreads quote list is unchanged and unrelated).
