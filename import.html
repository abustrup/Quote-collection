    How to make the PDFs
    1. Open goodreads.com/quotes/list while signed in.
    2. Print the page – Cmd-P on a Mac, Ctrl-P otherwise – and choose Save as PDF as the destination. The headers, footers and page numbers the browser adds are expected; they get stripped here.
    3. Click through to the next page of the list and repeat, once per page. The list holds thirty quotes to a page.
    4. Drop all the files above. Importing the same page twice is harmless: a quote already in the collection is recognised and left alone.
    Read entirely in your browser. No upload, no network, no account.
